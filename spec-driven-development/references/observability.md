# Observability and Operational Readiness Checklist

Use this when the change affects production behavior, debugging, supportability, or incident response.

## Design coverage

- logs, metrics, traces, and dashboards affected or required
- new failure signals and alert conditions
- support and debugging workflow impact
- ownership and on-call visibility

## Requirement coverage

- required telemetry
- required alerts or dashboards
- post-deploy verification and supportability expectations

## Task coverage

- metric, log, and trace updates
- dashboard or alert changes
- runbook or support-note updates
